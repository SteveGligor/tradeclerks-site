## 2. What Trade Clerks does

Trade Clerks turns what you say or type about a job into an invoice, starts jobs from your customers' texts, sends invoices to your customers by email, and keeps track of your jobs, customers and addresses. On plans that include it, Trade Clerks also gives you a business phone number for texting with your customers. Where available, it also helps you record installed equipment and track warranty registrations and rebate or incentive claims.

We're improving the app all the time, so features may change, be added or be removed.
