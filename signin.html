Sign in on your iPhone

The sign-in email works in the Trade Clerks app. Nothing on this page signs you in.

On your iPhone, with the app

Go back to Trade Clerks and enter the code from the same email on the Check your email screen.

If that screen is closed, tap Email me a sign-in link on the Sign in screen to get a new email.

On your iPhone, without the app

Trade Clerks isn't in the App Store yet.

On a computer

Open the email on your iPhone and tap Sign in there, or enter its code in the app.

The button and the code are for one sign-in, and both stop working after an hour.
