Support

Email support@tradeclerks.com. A real person replies within two business days.

Delete your account

In the app: Menu → Delete account. It can't be undone. Invoices you have already emailed stay with the people you sent them to. The Privacy Policy lists what's deleted.

If you can't get into the app, email privacy@tradeclerks.com.

Get your records out

In the app: Menu → Year-end export, on every account. You can export a year of invoices as PDFs, with spreadsheets of your invoices, line items, customers and jobs (including a version for QuickBooks). The export is made on your phone; we don't keep a copy.

Straight answers

Will it make up a price?

No. If you didn't say a price, the line says "Add price" and the invoice can't go out. Hours and quantities aren't guessed either. You see every line before you tap Send.

What if I've got no signal?

If the signal drops, your recording is saved on your phone, ready to send when you're back in range.

Is it on Android?

Not yet. It's iPhone only to start.

Does it work with QuickBooks?

Keep QuickBooks if you need it. The year-end export has a version for QuickBooks. Live sync isn't built yet.

Is it only for gas and HVAC?

No. Invoices, quotes, the calendar and repeat jobs work for any trade. The warranty part is for HVAC and gas, water heaters included.

Where does my customers' information go?

Your information and your customers' is never sold. Recordings aren't kept on our servers or used to train AI. Delete your account and everything in it is deleted, as the Privacy Policy says.
